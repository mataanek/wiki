# Ideation: Hermes Integrations - Discord

**Source:** https://x.com/akshay_pachaar/status/2056356792494682385

**Core Insight:** Discord integration gives Hermes superpowers as described.

## The 1 Points

> 1. Discord: Ideal for channel-based automation. Hermes can be plugged into specific channels with dedicated workflows in each. Support tickets from email can be scanned, categorized, and dropped into an organized channel every morning without anyone lifting a finger.

## Ways to Implement & Options

### 1. Discord
- *Ways:* Use official APIs or plugins to integrate with Hermes; use webhooks or custom connectors; leverage existing community integrations.
- *Options:* Official SDKs, third-party libraries, self-hosted instances, cloud-hosted services.
- *High‑level Plan:* 
    Month 1: Research and prototype basic integration.
    Month 2: Develop and test the integration in a sandbox.
    Month 3: Deploy to production and gather user feedback.
- *Alternatives:* Use alternative services that provide similar functionality (e.g., for Obsidian: Logseq, Notion; for Reddit: Twitter, Hacker News; etc.).
