# St. Kilian Rich & Smoky (2022)

The St. Kilian distillery from Franconia delivers the smoky counterpart to the Mild & Fruity with the Classic Rich & Smoky. Matured in a combination of 70% ex-bourbon and 30% PX and oloroso sherry casks, this single malt promises the marriage of sweet fruit and elegant smoke – and delivers on that promise convincingly.
