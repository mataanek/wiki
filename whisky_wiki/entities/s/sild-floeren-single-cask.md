# Sild Flören (2018/2023) Single Cask 5 Years

Sild Flören is distilled in the renowned Lantenhammer distillery in Bavaria and then stored under very special conditions on the North Sea island of Sylt. Rumour has it that part of the maturation process even took place on ships, which gives this whisky a touch of adventure and maritime flair. With its 50.10 % vol. it has plenty of strength without being overpowering.
