# Port Charlotte 10 Years

## Overview
Port Charlotte 10 Years is a heavily peated single malt from Bruichladdich distillery on Islay, bottled at 50% ABV.

## Tasting Notes
**Nose:** Intense peat smoke, seaweed, brine, with hints of citrus and vanilla.
**Palate:** Rich and oily, with layers of smoke, medicinal notes, dark chocolate, and dried fruit.
**Finish:** Long, smoky, and warming with lingering maritime characteristics.

## Review
Score: 9/10 - An excellent heavily peated whisky with great complexity.
Value for money: 7/10 - Outstanding quality but at a premium price point.
