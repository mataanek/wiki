# Tamnavulin 11 Years (2013/2025) - James Eadie

This 11-year-old single malt from the Tamnavulin distillery presents an intense and rich aroma that immediately captivates. The complexity of the taste unfolds on the palate with a harmonious balance of fruity notes and a malty depth reminiscent of the cold season. The well-integrated sherry aromas add a classic touch to the experience, while the pleasant warmth in the finish gently caresses and creates space for subtle bitterness. A whisky that stimulates the senses and invites you to linger, even if it may lack that certain something to stand out from others.
