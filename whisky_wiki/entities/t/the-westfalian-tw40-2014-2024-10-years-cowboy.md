# The Westfalian TW40

The Westfalian TW40 is one of the first 10-years whiskies from Germany, presented by the renowned Northoff Feinbrennerei in cooperation with the The Westfalian. This Single Corn Whisky is matured in a fresh cask and boasts an impressive high proof of 69.60% ABV—almost a hazmat experience—despite its relatively mature age of 10 years.
