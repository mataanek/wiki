# Laphroaig 10 Years

Laphroaig 10 Year Old is the distillery's core expression, embodying the quintessential Islay single malt character. Made from 100% Islay barley, peated to approximately 20-25 phenol parts per million (ppm), and matured exclusively in ex-bourbon casks, this whisky delivers the intense, medicinal profile that Laphroaig is renowned for.
