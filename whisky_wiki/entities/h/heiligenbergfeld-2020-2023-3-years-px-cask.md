# HeiligenBergFeld 3 Years (2020/2023) PX Cask

Located in the picturesque Five-Lakes region of the Starnberg district, the HeiligenBergFeld Distillery was established in 2017 and has since specialized in single cask bottlings. The HeiligenBergFeld 2020/2023, bottled at 57.9% ABV, matured for three years in a Pedro-Ximénez sherry cask, offering a glimpse into the burgeoning craftsmanship of this Bavarian distillery.
