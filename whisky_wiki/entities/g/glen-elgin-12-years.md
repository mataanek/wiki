# Glen Elgin 12 Years

Glen Elgin, one of Scotland's lesser-known distilleries, has been distilling whisky since around 1900. As part of the Classic Malts Selection, it becomes even more interesting when viewed in the context of the full lineup. After being closed several times, the distillery has been producing its own bottlings since its reopening in 1998, in addition to a lion's share which is processed in blends.Apart from special releases and anniversary bottlings, the 12-years single malt is the only permanently available bottling of the distillery. A reason to try it.
