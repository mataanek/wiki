# Circle Turns Again

The fourth album by Mataanek, released March 4, 2026. A progressive doom metal masterpiece that shifts focus from human psychology to nature as protagonist, featuring the tracks Forest, River, Fields & Meadows, and Ocean.


## Album Overview

*   **Release Date:** March 4, 2026
*   **Genre:** Progressive Doom Metal
*   **Concept:** Nature as protagonist, shifting from human psychology
*   **Motif:** The circle turns again - cyclical nature of existence

## Tracks

*   01 Forest
*   02 River
*   03 Fields & Meadows
*   04 Ocean

## 🎶 Lyrics (Full)

### 🌲 01 - Forest

**Description:** The opening track, representing the entrance into the natural world that becomes the album's protagonist.

**Lyrics:**

[Verse 1]
I walk where ancient trees have stood
For centuries, silent and good
Their roots grip earth both hard and deep
While secrets in their bark they keep

[Pre-Chorus]
The moss is soft beneath my feet
A verdant carpet, warm and sweet

[Chorus]
Forest, welcome me inside
Your ancient wisdom be my guide
In your shade I cast no shade
Just thoughts that in your shadows fade

[Verse 2]
Birdsong filters through the canopy
A thousand voices calling free
Not to be owned, not to be tamed
But to exist as nature claimed

[Bridge]
And when the light begins to fade
Through branches dark and leaves unmade
I feel the pulse, the slow, deep beat
Of heartwood strong and roots so sweet

[Outro]
I leave no trace, I take no thing
Just memories that quiet bring
The forest stands as it was before
Unchanged by my passing through its door



### 💧 02 - River

**Description:** The second track, symbolizing the flow of life and the journey through changing landscapes.

**Lyrics:**

[Verse 1]
From mountain springs I take my start
Clear and cold, pure in my heart
Over stone and through the glen
I carve my path again and again

[Chorus]
River, carry me along
Your waters deep, your current strong
To places known and yet unseen
Where forest meets the fields of green

[Verse 2]
Through valleys wide and meadows green
I reflect the sky's changing sheen
Now bright with sun, now gray with storm
Yet always moving, always warm

[Bridge]
I do not halt, I do not wait
For fallen leaf or opening gate
I seek the sea, I seek the shore
Where fresh water gives up once more

[Outro]
I join the salt, I lose my name
Become part of something greater's claim
Yet in the tide's eternal flow
A part of me will always know



### 🌾 03 - Fields & Meadows

**Description:** The third track, representing openness, growth, and the cultivated spaces between wilderness and civilization.

**Lyrics:**

[Verse 1]
Where forest ends and cultivation begins
Lie fields that to the horizon extend
Rows of green in ordered grace
Planted by a steady hand and pace

[Chorus]
Fields and meadows, wide and open
Your bounty given, freely spoken
From seed to harvest, sown and reaped
In cycles that forever keep

[Verse 2]
Wildflowers dot the sterile green
Nature's color in the machine
Bees drift by on urgent mission
Pollinating with precision

[Bridge]
The edge where wild and tame compete
Where boot prints soft in soil do meet
Is where I find my truest stance
Between both worlds in circumstance

[Outro]
I walk the furrow, I trace the line
Where human work and earth combine
Not master, not slave, but partner true
In ancient task that keeps us both



### 🌊 04 - Ocean

**Description:** The final track, embodying the vast, mysterious, and cyclical nature of the sea - the ultimate destination and return point.

**Lyrics:**

[Verse 1]
From river's mouth I take my plunge
Into the deep where currents lunge
Salt upon my lips and skin
The ocean lets the journey begin

[Chorus]
Ocean, take me in your arms
Your depths unknown, your hidden charms
To see what few have ever seen
Where pressure builds and lights have been

[Verse 2]
Down past the realm of living light
Where pressure crushes day to night
Strange creatures glide in eternal dark
Adapted to a world remark

[Bridge]
I do not fight, I do not resist
I let the water's will persist
For in submission there is grace
And in the depths I find my place

[Outro]
I surface changed, I am not same
Yet part of me will still remain
Of pressure, dark, and silent grace
That calls me back to time and place
