01 - Therapy in Despair

[Intro]

[Verse]  
The sea forgot my name again,  
I left it carved in salt and shame.  
Every wave repeats what I destroyed,  
A voice that fades before it’s heard.

[Chorus]  
And I still ache for what should be gone,  
The ghost that hums the same old song.  
A year of empty dawns,  
Still waiting for a scar to heal.

[Verse]  
The mirror mocks my fragile spine,  
A hollow frame where warmth declined.  
You left no flame, just static air,  
And echoes peeling from thin despair.

[Bridge]  
Somewhere under this cloudy sky, 
I still don’t know why.  
The silence wears a known shape,  
A cruel, familiar lie.

[Verse]  
I have to concentrate on something—  
This is my therapy, my release.  
Building something from deep inside,  
Just to prove I can still endure.

[Chorus]  
All that I touch turns grey again,  
Still burning slow beneath the rain.  
The heart forgets, the bones recall—  
I left, but the weight won’t fall.

[Verse]  
What next? Live, and how?  
The hours crawl, I just allow.  
Breathing through what won’t let go,  
A quiet war no one will know.

[Outro]  
Just me, the sound, and empty air,  
Therapy in despair.
