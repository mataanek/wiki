     134|3. The Music That Counts the Stars
     135|This third track zooms all the way out: from self, through society, to the cosmos and whatever is behind it all. The focus is on insignificance, myth, and the question of whether any of this is seen by anything beyond us
     136|Theme is humanity as a brief, noisy glitch in an ancient, mostly indifferent universe. Even if You love life, when you look up, you see how small and possibly alone we are… or worse: not alone, just unnoticed. Blend scientific/cosmic imagery (void, stars, entropy) with hints of old gods, creators, or primordial forces that may or may not care.
